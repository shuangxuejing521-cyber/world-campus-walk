Version 2 (26 November 2026). Revised after feedback from a university department.

Sent from the founder's personal address. One email per university, in the university's language where we have it (Korean, Chinese), otherwise English. **Prefer writing to student clubs** (3D modelling, architecture, game development, Minecraft) rather than to offices. Do not tell a university that it is "already on the globe" or imply that it takes part.

## 中文

**主题：** 邀请贵校同学在“世界校园漫游”上建造自己的校园

您好，

我是景双雪，韩国外国语大学中语中文系博士生。很多人一生都没有机会走进自己梦想中的大学，所以我发起了“世界校园漫游”：在一个 3D 地球上，去不了的人也能以第一人称走进世界各地的大学校园。

这是我个人发起、志愿运营的非商业项目，尚未注册为任何机构，没有经费和收入，与任何大学都没有隶属关系。

项目的规则只有一条：**每所大学的校园，由这所学校自己的学生来建。** 用什么方式建都可以。几点承诺：

- **版权归作者**（按学校规定归学校的，就归学校）。项目只获得在本站展示的许可，默认不允许他人下载或再利用。
- 学校或团队可以随时发邮件要求更正或下架，7 天内处理。
- 不收费，不放广告，不出售或授权任何模型。

目前能走进的只有韩国外国语大学的示范校园，由我本人制作。贵校目前没有参与本项目。如果贵校有同学愿意来建，我们非常欢迎。

## English

**Subject:** Invitation: your students could build [University]'s campus on World Campus Walk

Hello,

I'm Jing Shuangxue, a PhD student in Chinese Language and Literature at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies in Seoul. Many people never get the chance to visit the university they dream of, so I started World Campus Walk: a 3D globe where anyone who cannot go can walk through university campuses in first person.

It is a non-commercial project that I started personally and run as a volunteer. It is not a registered organisation, has no funding or income, and is not affiliated with any university.

The project has one rule: **every campus is built by that university's own students**, by any method they choose. Our commitments:

- **Copyright stays with the authors** (or with their university, where its rules say so). The project only receives permission to display the model on its site; by default nobody else may download or reuse it.
- A university or team can ask for correction or removal by email at any time, and we act within 7 days.
- No fees, no advertising, and no model is ever sold or licensed.

At present the only walkable campus is a demonstration of Hankuk University of Foreign Studies, which I made myself. [University] does not currently take part in the project. If any of your students would like to build it, they would be very welcome.
