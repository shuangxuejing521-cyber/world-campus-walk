# Licence for campus models

Campus models are **not** published under a single licence.

- Copyright in each model stays with its authors (or their university).
- Each team chooses its own public licence: display only, all rights reserved (default), CC BY-NC 4.0, or CC BY 4.0.
- The licence of each campus is stated in the `licence` field of its `campus.json`.
- Display-only models are not stored in this public repository.
